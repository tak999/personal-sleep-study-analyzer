# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

## Personal use and data collected

Personal Sleep & Study Analyzer is a tool used only by its owner. It retrieves data that the owner authorizes through Oura's authorization screen. This may include sleep, activity, readiness, heart rate, and other information provided by the API within the permissions granted.

## Purpose and storage

Retrieved data is stored on the owner's Mac and used to review lifestyle habits and study plans. Authentication credentials are protected separately from health data and are not stored in this public repository.

## AI-assisted analysis and disclosure

At the owner's explicit direction, data needed for analysis may be provided to an AI service. In that case, the service's terms and privacy policy apply. Data is not used for advertising, sold, or made publicly available to third parties.

## Retention and deletion

Locally stored data is retained until the owner deletes it. The owner may revoke access through Oura to stop future retrieval. Revoking access does not automatically delete data already stored locally.
